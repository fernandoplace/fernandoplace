About HPC

High Performance Computing (HPC) combines multiple compute resources to solve complex scientific, engineering and business problems. HPC environments provide large-scale parallel processing capabilities, enabling workloads that would otherwise take days, weeks or months to complete on traditional systems.

This dashboard provides a simple view of welfare workload trends, yearly statistics and runtime metrics stored within Google Sheets.
