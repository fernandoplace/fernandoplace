Old Gate Photos

The original Fernando Place gate stood for several decades, quietly welcoming families, visitors, and generations who grew up here. Though worn and weathered with time, it carried a beautiful history — a reminder of how our community began and how far it has come. Renovating the gate was not just a repair project; it was a way of preserving that legacy while giving our neighbourhood the safe, dignified entrance it deserves today.
